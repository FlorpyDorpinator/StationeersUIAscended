# Stationeers UI Ascended 1.0 is out!
A UI overhaul that rebuilds Stationeers' inventory and HUD around your two hands.

**What you get**
- **Radial wheels** - middle mouse for your tool belt, Tab for your bags + search, R for the item in your hand (every setting, scroll to adjust), 1-6 for each piece of gear
- **Two hands, no hotbar, ever** - hands, batteries and logistics stay; menu-diving goes
- **Universal Inventory (B)** - every bag you carry in one window: drag & drop, pin bags out, Shift+drag moves a whole item type, drag straight into chargers and lockers
- **Smart Stow (G) that remembers** - items go back to the exact bag and slot they came from
- **Curved visor HUD** - compass, vitals with a live hologram portrait; real numbers need a powered suit, and it glitches as your battery dies
- **4 themes + the F9 HUD Designer** - move, resize and restyle anything, live
- **F10 Control Center** - every setting with search, and every key rebindable
- **Report bugs in-game** - F10 > Suggestions/Bugs goes straight to the devs, and you can track it there
- **In-game tutorial** - 19 short lessons: take the guided tour or learn as you go
- **Multiplayer-friendly** - every inventory action uses the game's own server-checked path

**Get it:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3776545141
Needs **BepInEx 5 + StationeersLaunchPad**. In game: **F10** for settings, **F9** for the HUD Designer.
